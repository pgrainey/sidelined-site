Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Sidelined is an iPhone app that blocks betting apps and websites that you choose. This policy explains what we collect and what we do not.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The apps, categories and websites you pick in Apple's Screen Time picker. Apple gives the app opaque tokens, so we never learn which apps you chose. They are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your lock settings, schedule, teams, streak and block counts. Block counts are approximate and are kept as timestamps on your device.

What we collect

  • Product analytics (PostHog). A few coarse events: onboarding finished, apps picked, lock turned on, blocked screen shown, unlock attempted, trial started and purchase. Events carry no app names, no browsing data and no Screen Time data. They are tied to a random anonymous identifier, not your name or email.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple). If you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status so the app can unlock Pro. We never see your card or Apple ID.
  • Hosted lists. The app downloads our website block list and game schedule. These requests are ordinary web requests and contain no personal data from you.

What we do not do

  • No account, no email and no bank connection.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and sites.
  • We do not use Screen Time information for anything except applying the blocks you ask for on your own device.

Screen Time access

Sidelined asks for Screen Time (Family Controls) access for yourself, on your own device. You can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings. Turning it off stops blocking.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics or purchase records tied to your anonymous identifier, contact us at the address below.

Children

Sidelined is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
